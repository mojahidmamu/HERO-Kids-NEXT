## Introduction

As a parent, your child's safety is always the top priority. With so many toys available in the market, it can be overwhelming to choose the right ones. This guide will help you make informed decisions when selecting toys for your little ones.

---

## 1. Check for Age Appropriateness

Always look for age recommendations on toy packaging. Toys designed for older children may contain small parts that can be choking hazards for younger kids.

**Tip:** Follow the manufacturer's age guidelines carefully.

---

## 2. Look for Non-Toxic Materials

Ensure that toys are made from non-toxic, BPA-free, and lead-free materials. Look for certifications like **ASTM**, **EN71**, or **CE** marks that indicate safety compliance.

**Tip:** Avoid toys with strong chemical odors — they may contain harmful substances.

---

## 3. Avoid Small Parts for Young Children

For children under 3 years old, avoid toys with small detachable parts. Use a **choke tube** to test if a toy part is too small.

**Tip:** Coins, marbles, and small magnets are common choking hazards.

---

## 4. Check for Sharp Edges and Points

Inspect toys for sharp edges, points, or rough surfaces that could cause cuts or injuries. Wooden toys should be smooth and splinter-free.

**Tip:** Run your fingers along the edges to feel for sharpness.

---

## 5. Ensure Sturdy Construction

Toys should be well-made and durable. Cheaply made toys may break easily, creating sharp edges or small parts.

**Tip:** Pull and tug on parts to check if they are securely attached.

---

## 6. Avoid Toys with Long Strings or Cords

Toys with long strings, cords, or ribbons can pose a strangulation risk. Keep them away from young children.

**Tip:** Choose toys with short, sturdy handles instead.

---

## 7. Check for Flammability

Ensure toys are made from flame-resistant materials. Look for labels that indicate compliance with safety standards.

**Tip:** Keep toys away from open flames and heat sources.

---

## 8. Be Cautious with Magnets and Batteries

Toys containing small magnets or button batteries can be dangerous if swallowed. Choose toys with secure battery compartments.

**Tip:** Use toys with child-safe battery covers.

---

## 9. Read Online Reviews

Before buying, read reviews from other parents. They can provide insights about safety, durability, and overall quality.

**Tip:** Look for reviews that mention safety issues.

---
